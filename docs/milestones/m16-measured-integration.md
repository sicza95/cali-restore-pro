## Integrated workflows

- Estimate phases select a surface, current assessment or named checkpoint, and a committed phase layer. Editing and independent layer cloning take place in the surface editor. The estimate worker uses calibrated crop/master intersections and preserves the project, surface, photo, assessment, preparation and calibration revision in the quantity provenance. Time mode retains entered hours and requires acknowledgement when measured area changes. Productivity mode recalculates. Pending marks and invalid calibration cannot supply an area. Previously issued versions remain immutable.
- Reports select the current mapping assessment or named checkpoints and can compare two selected versions. Preview and export share the same measurement rows, including assessment provenance, calibrated extent, converted areas, coverage and primary-minus-comparison totals. Stored sample selectors migrate to current/no comparison. A missing real checkpoint stays unavailable.
- PDF, SVG and PNG use the assessment's committed visible layer styles, including embedded repeating patterns. Map vectors are clipped to the assessed area before encoding. Selected reference attachments are downsampled to at most 640 pixels on the long side and re-encoded as JPEG. CSV and XLSX retain structured measurement rows and selected reference ordinals. Editable XLSX uses the issued inputs, area and assessment label. An issued quote is included only when its surfaces and measured assessment selections are in report scope.
- Ordinary previews and downloads remain labelled drafts, including saved unissued estimates (MEAS-06). Explicit final issuance is described below. Existing account-local report authorization and the assessment, summary and full estimate grants continue to apply.

## OUT-11 · Final report issuance (2026-11-17, part of #54)

The owner decision permits final reports only from an issued, complete estimate. Partial totals remain drafts. `src/platform/reports/issuance.ts` implements the shared rule: include at least one surface and a selected estimate, require its issued flag and complete calculation with an available total, and include every estimated surface and measured assessment version. Earlier issued versions remain selectable; a newer draft does not replace their totals or provenance.

Reports and Outputs offer **Issue final report** with a visible, accessible EN/HU explanation when blocked. The action re-reads the saved estimate, prepares the selected content, checks the rule again, and saves the report settings, project revision, issuance time and source estimate ID/version/date before opening its final preview. Save failures do not open a final preview. This stores the latest report issuance, not an append-only report history. Saving a report draft replaces that record with a draft. Restoring a backup into a new project clears final issuance; that copy must be issued explicitly.

Final previews and all five encoders use final labels and source issued estimate version/date plus report issuance time; PDF and XLSX downloads have final filenames. Values-only and editable workbooks use the same source record. Editable cells may subsequently change in a spreadsheet application; final provenance identifies the issued source, not later external edits. Preview draft remains an independent path and retains draft labels and filenames even after a final report was issued.

Evidence: `src/platform/reports/issuance.test.ts` covers missing, unissued, partial, out-of-scope and historical sources, EN/HU labels, inconsistent provenance rejection and restored copies. `tests-platform/measured-reports.browser.ts` with `final-report.ts` exercises blocked assessment selection, persisted issuance, final PDF/XLSX downloads, both iPad orientations, HU report content and returning to a draft download. `incomplete-assessment.browser.ts` verifies the visible EN/HU reason while preserving partial draft workbook exports. These use synthetic local fixtures; they do not qualify physical print scale, target spreadsheet recalculation or physical Safari/Pencil behavior.
