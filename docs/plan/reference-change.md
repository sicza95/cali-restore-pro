# Reference change with drafts

16 November 2026 · Status: done · Issue: #75 · Found while doing [the draft source on the project page](draft-source.md) (#72)

Goal: while the drawing drafts of a surface are pending, no photo change hides them or lets their photo be removed, on the surface page and on the project page, and drafts already hidden can always be brought back and resolved.

## Where things stand

On `main` at `d0c80f7`, the surface page opens the journal of its reference only: the first photo with the mapping role (`reference()` in `SurfaceWorkspace.tsx`). While that journal has drafts, `ProjectOriginals` disables the reference's "Remove" and role select with the `draftPhoto` reason (`pendingSource`, on the project page through `draftSource` since #72). No other photo is blocked.

**Reproduced** in Chrome against `d0c80f7`, with a temporary spec outside the repo, on both pages: a new project with `early.png` attached as "Reference only" and then `main.png` as "Mapping"; a pending mark on `main.png`. `main.png`'s "Remove" and role select are disabled with the reason, `early.png`'s controls are enabled. Setting `early.png` to "Mapping" (on the surface page in one run, on the project page in the other) saves, and then `main.png`'s "Remove" and role select are enabled with no reason; on the surface page the drawing shows `early.png`'s layers (the master mask only, no "Damage mask" and no pending mark). Removing `main.png` succeeds, and after setting `early.png` back to "Reference only" the surface has no mapping photo and no layers: `main.png`'s assessment is gone and its journal, with the pending mark, has nothing that reads it. No warning at any step. As the issue says.

## Findings

- **Every way the reference can change.** The reference is the first image of `surface.images` with the role `mapping`, in attach order. `surface.images` is written only by `ProjectOriginals` (attach, role change, remove) on both pages, and wholesale by a project replaced from elsewhere.
  - Giving the mapping role to a photo listed before the reference. Every such photo is "Reference only" (otherwise it would be the reference), so its role select can only make it the reference. **Blocked** while the reference's journal holds drafts: the select is disabled with the `draftPhoto` reason ("Resolve the drawing drafts before changing this original.", true here: the drafts must be resolved first).
  - Changing the reference's own role, or removing it: blocked since #72, unchanged.
  - Changing the role of a photo listed after the reference: the first mapping photo stays the same. **Harmless.**
  - Removing a photo listed before the reference: those are "Reference only", so the first mapping photo stays the same. An earlier mapping photo cannot exist. **Harmless.**
  - Attaching: always appended (`[...images, { id, role }]`), so a new mapping photo becomes the reference only when the surface has no mapping photo, and then there is no reference whose drafts could be pending. **Harmless.**
  - Reordering: there is no reorder control. **None.**
  - Not an action on this device's originals: another device's revision (sync "Keep cloud" replaces the project row and leaves the journals) and older data, including data saved before #72, when the project page let the draft source be re-roled. Backup restores create a new project with new photo ids and no journals; checkpoint restores change the assessment, not the photos. These are why photos whose journal holds drafts can exist besides the reference: the next point.
- **Hidden drafts.** A photo that is not the reference can hold drafts in its stored journal (older data, another device's revision). Its role can be "Mapping" (listed after the reference) or "Reference only". Removing it drops its assessment and orphans its journal: **blocked** (owner's direction 1 is approved; this second rule is the orchestrator's default for the owner, not yet approved by the owner). Its role select stays enabled, because it is the way to bring the drafts back.
- **The way out.** The drafts of a photo show again once it is the first mapping photo; the surface page then opens its journal, where they are saved or discarded ("Discard draft" for a conflict). To get there: if the photo is "Reference only", give it the mapping role; if it is "Mapping" behind the reference, set the photos before it to "Reference only". The only control that can be blocked on that path is a role select blocked by this rule or #72, and both lift once the current reference's drafts are saved or discarded on the surface page, which is always possible. So there is no state without a way out. An unreadable journal blocks nothing, as before. The new reason says where to go: "This original has drawing drafts. To resolve them, make it the first original with the Mapping role." (new `hiddenDraftPhoto` in `project-copy.ts`, EN and HU; the `draftPhoto` text would be true but gives no way to find drafts the user cannot see).
- **Which journals are read.** Not only mapping photos: a "Reference only" photo can hold drafts (older data, another device's revision, and a hidden source re-roled under the rule above), and reading only mapping photos would leave its "Remove" free, the bug again. So every photo of the surface is read. A photo without a journal costs one IndexedDB get; one with a journal is decrypted and parsed. Project page: every photo of the selected surface, read again after every save (the project revision), on selection change and on remount (#72's key, unchanged). Surface page: every photo except the reference, whose drafts come from the open journal as before; keyed the same way. On both pages the originals' controls are held while the read runs, and an unreadable journal counts as none.

## Approach

1. **Platform** (`surface-attachments.ts`). `draftPhotos(readJournal, scope, images)` returns the ids whose stored journal holds drafts; it replaces `draftSource`. `draftBlocks(images, drafts)` is the rule: the reference with drafts blocks its "Remove" and role (#72) and the role of every photo before it (#75); any other photo with drafts blocks its "Remove" only, with the hidden-drafts reason.
2. **Pages.** `createDraftPhotos` (`src/ui/projects/`) reads `draftPhotos` in a resource for both pages; the surface page adds the reference from its open journal. `ProjectOriginals` takes the set of draft photos instead of `pendingSource` and maps `draftBlocks` to `disabled` and `title`, the reason mechanism as today (#79 changes it later).
3. **Copy.** `hiddenDraftPhoto` in EN and HU.

The #72 `draftSource` cases move to `draftPhotos`. Each run against the old code or a mutant to see it fail.
- Browser, in the projects flow through `tests-platform/draft-source.ts` (`projects.browser.ts` stays at 399 lines), on a new surface without checkpoints where a "Reference only" photo is listed before the mapping photo: with a pending mark, the earlier photo's role select is disabled with the `draftPhoto` description on both pages, its "Remove" is free; enabled again after the draft is saved and after it is discarded. With another device's revision that makes the draft source "Reference only", its "Remove" is disabled with the new reason and its role select is enabled on both pages; giving it the mapping role brings the pending mark back, and discarding it frees the controls. Run against the old code to see each fail.

## Outcome

The reason stays a `title`; shown as visible text since #79 ([plan](visible-reason.md)). `ProjectDetail.tsx` is at 305 lines, `SurfaceWorkspace.tsx` at 388, `ProjectOriginals.tsx` at 198.
- The browser checks are `verifyReferenceChange` in `tests-platform/reference-change.ts`, called at the end of `verifyDraftSourceBlocked`; the originals helpers moved to `tests-platform/originals.ts`. `projects.browser.ts` stays at 399 lines. With the code before the fix, the flow fails at its first check (the earlier photo's role on the surface page has no description). Mutants fail as follows: without the earlier-photo rule, at the same check, and at the project-page check when the surface-page one is skipped; without the hidden-drafts rule, reading mapping photos only, or the surface page reading no stored journals, at the surface-page hidden-drafts check; with the project page reading the first mapping photo only (#72), at the project-page hidden-drafts check; with the hidden photo's role blocked too, at its "enabled" check. The Vitest cases fail on mutants reading mapping photos only, without the error handling, without either rule, blocking later photos' roles, taking the first photo as the reference, or blocking a hidden photo's role.
- Found on the way: `SurfacePhoto` threw when a photo finished loading after the shown photo had changed (`onLoad` read the cleared image). The new checks switch photos right after opening the surface page and hit it in repeated runs; the handler now ignores such a load.
